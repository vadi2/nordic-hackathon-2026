How scoring works

Fix conformance first, because any error or fatal validation issue scores your submission 0. A valid bundle earns 50, plus +10 per distinct Composition section that actually has entries (capped at +160), and +2 for every clinical resource outside the Composition and Patient (capped at +60). Sections only count once - repeating a section or padding the bundle will not make it richer. Your company score is your best patient summary, plus a one-time +20 "first pass" bonus for the first company to submit a valid one, plus +15 for each other EHDS document type you also get passing. Interop testing is not scored.

Other EHDS documents we also validate4

This is the patient summary track, and the summary is what the leaderboard ranks. But EHDS (Regulation (EU) 2025/327, Article 14) names six priority categories of health data that EHR systems have to exchange, so if you bring one of the others it is detected from the bundle and validated properly against its own HL7 Europe guide. Each one you pass is worth a flat +15.

Part of the Nordic Hackathon 2026, 18-20 November in Kista.

  
Bundles are validated by Matchbox, the open-source FHIR validation and
  mapping server from ahdis,
  which runs the official HL7 FHIR validator underneath.

  
Structure, profiles and cardinality are checked in full, against the
  HL7 EU EPS guide - or, for one of the other EHDS document
  types, against its own HL7 Europe guide. External code systems those
  guides do not themselves carry and that require a license (such as SNOMED CT,
  LOINC, WHO ATC) are not checked.

  
Runs on Provbana, built by Vadim Peretokin.
